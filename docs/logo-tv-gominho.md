# Logo TV Gominho

Logo criado com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens (ImageGen), com fundo transparente, para a tela de entrada.

Revisão a partir das duas referências fornecidas pelo usuário: letras geométricas em bloco, coloridas e com extrusão 3D, com uma TV de tubo prateada sobre o nome. Sem frutas ou elementos cítricos. O emblema usa gabinete retangular, antenas e botões próprios.

Arquivo: `pacotes/interface/src/assets/tv-gominho-logo.webp` (960 × 640, WebP com canal alfa, ~80 KB; convertido do PNG original de 1536 × 1024, que fica fora do repositório para o app continuar leve).

## Prompt utilizado

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished original logo for "TV GOMINHO", a nostalgic Brazilian cartoon video player. Transparent background with real alpha.
Centered composition: a silver retro CRT television ABOVE a single line reading exactly "TV GOMINHO". Typography is heavy geometric uppercase sans serif, tightly spaced, slightly forward leaning, clean sharp letter construction with extruded 3D sides. Not rounded bubble or candy lettering. Exact spelling: TV G-O-M-I-N-H-O. TV is red; G yellow; O lime; M green; I cyan; N blue; H violet; O purple. Bold and legible.
The emblem is an ORIGINAL silver CRT television cabinet with softly rounded rectangular corners, short twin rabbit-ear antennas, two small circular tuning knobs on its right side, and a luminous curved screen showing horizontal rainbow scanlines (violet, blue, cyan, green, yellow, red). The rainbow screen is uninterrupted, without a central sphere. It is clearly a little television set, not a spherical broadcaster emblem. The television is centered over the wordmark and about 60 percent as wide as the text. A tiny subtle embossed G on the silver bottom bezel.
Rendering: high-quality late-1990s/early-2000s television CGI with metallic silver highlights, pearl-white bevels, saturated rainbow letter faces, restrained dark sides. The reference mood is nostalgic children's morning TV. Compact balanced silhouette, all elements fully visible. Suitable on a dark navy website. No orange color, no fruit, no citrus, no food, no leaves. No existing broadcaster symbols. No extra text, slogan, background, floor, watermark, or painted checkerboard. One complete finished logo.
```
